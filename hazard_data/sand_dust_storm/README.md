# Sand and Dust Storm

## Data Source

**UNCCD Sand and Dust Storm Hazard Data**  
United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD)  
https://www.unccd.int/land-and-life/land-degradation-neutrality/related-tools/sand-and-dust-storms

## Metric

Frequency and intensity of sand and dust storm events. Captures areas prone to dust emission and transport events that affect air quality and children's respiratory health.

## Processing

Data is downloaded directly from the UNCCD source. No additional processing is applied.

**GEE asset:** `projects/unicef-ccri/assets/hazards/sand_dust_storm`

## Output Format

- GEE raster asset
- Spatial resolution: 1 km
- Coverage: global (arid and semi-arid regions primarily)

## Update Cadence

Updated when UNCCD releases new versions of the sand and dust storm hazard layer.
